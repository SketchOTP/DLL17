## Zero-inheritance restated at R001 close

At the R001 exit gate the greenfield claim is stronger than a historical one, and
also more load-bearing, because R001 is the first phase with real algorithms in
it.

Two of those algorithms are published work. Neither was copied: SHA-256 was
implemented from the FIPS 180-4 specification and verified against that
document's own published vectors, and SplitMix64 was implemented from the
published mixing function and constants. The distinction matters legally and
practically — implementing a described algorithm creates first-party source,
while pasting a reference implementation would inherit its licence terms into a
repository whose own licence is proprietary.

Everything else in the deterministic core — the canonical codec, the envelope,
the fixed-point arithmetic, the reducer, the journal, the replay kernel, the
Class W protocol, the panic witness and the migration framework — is original to
this repository.

No organism implementation or state exists to have been inherited. R001
implements determinism mechanism only; physiology, drives, learning, memory,
relationships and development remain unimplemented and gated.
