## Reproducing this bundle

```
python3 tools/build_qualification_bundle.py --verify
```

To reproduce the underlying evidence rather than just verify it, run
`./gradlew build`, `./gradlew :desktop-runner:run`,
`python3 tools/generate_lookup_tables.py --check`, and the cross-target
harness `tools/qualify_r001_determinism.sh` against each target.

Unlike the R000 device evidence, the determinism evidence **is**
expected to be byte-identical on every rerun and on every target. That
is the entire claim: if `R001_EVIDENCE_DIGEST` differs between two runs
or two targets, R001 has failed.
