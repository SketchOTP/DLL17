AUDITOR AUTHORITY (AuditorAuthorityPolicyV1)

  D016-I reclassifies both routed reviewers as adversarial auditors.
  Human evidence determines aliveness. Frozen math determines PASS/FAIL.
  Agents audit; they do not adjudicate.

  NO_AGENT_ADJUDICATES=true
  AUDITORS_MAY_RAISE_FINDINGS=true
  SEPARATION_HOLDS=true
  AUDITOR_AUTHORITY_POLICY_HOLDS=true
  supersedes: the D016-C reviewer-adjudicates model

  Structurally impossible for an auditor:
    - create a PASS — no finding type carries a verdict, and the adjudicator's pass conditions read recomputed statistics only
    - create a FAIL — an unconfirmed finding is inert, and an ambiguous one suspends for the Architect rather than deciding
    - rescue a failed result — findings are never inputs to the primary classification, which is a pure function of the screened human data
    - override the deterministic gate — the gate outcome is computed before dispositions are assigned, and no disposition can reopen it
    - confirm its own finding — confirmation is the adjudicator re-deriving the violation from evidence, never the auditor asserting it

  Machine-checkable violation codes: 16
    BASELINE_NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_QUALIFIED          baseline qualification evidence
    BASELINE_MARGIN_BELOW_FLOOR                   baseline margin vs BASELINE_COMPETENCE_MARGIN
    PILOT_NOT_PROTOCOL_VALID                      BlindVariancePilot.PilotRelease.protocolValid
    NON_SCORED_POOL_PARTICIPANT_ANALYSED          screening: PRIOR_NON_SCORED_POOL
    DUPLICATE_PARTICIPANT_ANALYSED                screening: DUPLICATE_PARTICIPANT
    INCOMPLETE_SESSION_ANALYSED                   screening: INCOMPLETE_SESSION
    SCORE_OUT_OF_RANGE_ANALYSED                   screening: SCORE_OUT_OF_RANGE
    SAMPLE_BELOW_POWERED_REQUIREMENT              A001FeasibilityBudget.requiredPairsPrimary
    FEASIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED                   A001FeasibilityBudget.FeasibilityState
    PROTOCOL_VERSION_MISMATCH                     A001StudyContract.PROTOCOL_VERSION
    INSTRUMENT_MISMATCH                           A001StudyContract.INSTRUMENT_ID
    ANALYSIS_VERSION_MISMATCH                     A001Analysis.ANALYSIS_VERSION
    THRESHOLD_WEAKENED_AFTER_FREEZE               frozen constants vs FROZEN_DECISION_THRESHOLDS
    ATTEMPT_BUDGET_EXCEEDED                       SpikeContract.MAX_SCORED_A001_ATTEMPTS
    ETHICS_DETERMINATION_ABSENT                   ethics determination in the gate evidence
    CLAIMED_OUTCOME_DISAGREES_WITH_RECOMPUTATION  recomputed primary classification

  A finding naming none of these is returned to the Architect rather than
  scored. A finding naming one of them is re-derived by the adjudicator
  from the evidence before it is allowed to matter, so an auditor that
  invents a violation, is talked into one by the material under review, or
  misses a real one cannot move the outcome in any direction.
