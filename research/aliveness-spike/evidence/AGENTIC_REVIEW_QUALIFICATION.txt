  Every reviewer exercised above is a deterministic in-repository fixture,
  not a language model. Repeated-run stability, position and order
  sensitivity, abstention rate and injection resistance are properties of a
  model, and no model was executed. The frozen thresholds above therefore
  have nothing to be applied to yet, which is exactly why they can be
  trusted not to have been fitted to a result.
