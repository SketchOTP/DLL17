## Information sheet

### What this is

We are researching whether a simulated creature seems alive to people. You will
meet two creatures, one after the other, and tell us how alive each one seemed
to you. There are no right or wrong answers and nothing about you is being
tested or measured.

### What you will do

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Reading this sheet and giving consent | about 5 minutes |
| A short briefing on how to interact | about 2 minutes |
| Meeting the first creature | 10 minutes |
| Rating the first creature | about 2 minutes |
| Meeting the second creature | 10 minutes |
| Rating the second creature | about 2 minutes |
| Two short closing questions | about 1 minute |
| Debrief, and any questions you have | about 5 minutes |
| **Total** | **about 37 minutes** |

With each creature you can touch it, call to it, offer it food, show it an
object, ignore it, or startle it. You can do any of those in any order, as
often as you like, or nothing at all. We will not ask you to follow a script.

### What we are asking you to rate

How alive the creature seemed to you. Not how good it looked, not how much you
liked it, and not whether the software worked.

### What we do not tell you until the end

The two creatures are driven by different software. We do not tell you which is
which, or in what way they differ, until the debrief — knowing would change what
you see. Both are shown to you in exactly the same way, for exactly the same
length of time, with exactly the same things you can do.

### Recordings

**No audio or video of you is recorded.** No camera or microphone is used at any
point.

What is recorded is what happened on screen: which of the six interactions you
chose and when, the creature's behaviour, and the two numbers you give us.

### What we collect about you

| Collected | Why |
|---|---|
| A participant number (e.g. `P-041`) | To pair your two ratings with each other |
| Your two 0–100 ratings | The study endpoint |
| Your two closing answers | Secondary questions |
| Timestamps and interaction events during the sessions | To check the session ran correctly and lasted the full time |
| Whether you have taken part in an earlier session of this research | You may only take part once |

| Not collected | |
|---|---|
| Your name, address, email or phone number, beyond what is needed to arrange and pay for the session, which is kept separately and destroyed after payment | |
| Your age, gender, occupation or any other demographic detail | |
| Audio, video or images of you | |
| Anything from your own device | |

### How long we keep it

The study data — your participant number, your ratings and your session events —
is kept indefinitely as part of the research record, because a negative result
must remain checkable and cannot be quietly deleted. It contains no information
that identifies you.

Your signed consent form contains your name and signature. It is an identifiable
record stored separately from both the enrolment register and study data; it
never contains or exposes the participant-number mapping. Signed consent is
retained for three years after completion of the A001 human-study programme and
then securely destroyed. This is a conservative project rule, not a claim that
45 CFR 46.115 legally governs this currently non-covered internal determination.

Any scheduling or payment identifier is kept separately from the study data and
destroyed after payment and eligibility checks are complete.

### Withdrawing

You can stop at any time, for any reason, without giving one, and without any
consequence. Just say so.

If you stop partway through, or ask us to, we delete your data. Once your
session is complete and your ratings have been merged into the analysis set, we
can still remove your record on request as long as the analysis has not been
run; after that the data is anonymous and we cannot identify which record is
yours in order to remove it.

### Payment

If a session is compensated, you are paid in full for attending, whatever you
answer, whether you complete the session, and whether you withdraw. Payment is
never contingent on your ratings. The amount, if any, is set by the study owner
and is stated on the booking, not here.

### Risks

We do not expect this to be distressing. One of the things you can do is startle
the creature, which will make it react as though frightened; some people find
that uncomfortable. You do not have to do it. You can stop at any time.

### Questions

The study operator can answer questions before, during or after the session.
Study or ethics questions and complaints may also be raised through the real
study-owner contact supplied in the booking/consent channel. A session cannot
begin unless that contact has been supplied.

## Debrief

Read to every participant, including those who withdraw.

> Thank you. Here is what we did not tell you beforehand.
>
> The two creatures you met run on different software. One is driven by a set of
> mechanisms we are researching — it learns what it likes, gets better at things
> it practises, becomes wary of things that have gone badly, and goes back to
> re-check things it had given up on. The other follows a detailed script written
> by hand: it is deliberately good, but it does not learn anything.
>
> We did not tell you which was which because knowing would have changed what you
> saw, and your unprompted impression is the whole point.
>
> You met the [first / second] creature as the learning one.
>
> Your two numbers go into a comparison across many participants. No single
> answer decides anything, and there was no answer we were hoping for — a result
> showing that the scripted creature seems just as alive is a real finding and
> will be published as one.
>
> Do you have any questions? Would you like the results when they are available?
