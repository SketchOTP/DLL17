# A001EvaluationContractV2

D016-L forward execution contract. This V2 document supersedes the human-study
execution path without deleting its V1 history.

The evaluation population is AI agents only. External human participants,
recruitment, compensation, consent, participant-hour calculations, the
36-person variance pilot, the 40-person baseline qualification, and
population-level human inference are prohibited for forward execution.

Stage 1 is a deterministic aggregation of a frozen panel of 12 independent
pairs (24 isolated executions), with counterbalanced candidate order and
neutral labels. Stage 1 uses the six frozen 0-100 rubric dimensions and
requires at least 9 position-consistent preferences and median overall-
aliveness delta of at least +10, after panel validity checks.

Stage 2 is one owner-only acceptance review on the intended Pixel. It is not a
statistical study and makes no population claim. It binds the exact commit,
APK, app/device identities, Android version, organism identity and timestamp,
and includes ordinary interaction plus a genuine lifecycle interruption.

Only AI qualification PASS plus owner Pixel PASS yields `A001_V2_PASS`.
Owner FAIL is load-bearing and cannot be overridden by any automated result.
Until both stages pass, `R003_R009=BLOCKED`.

At D016-L completion: AI formal executions are 0, owner reviews are 0, and
external human participants are 0.
