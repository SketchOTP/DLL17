## Goal

Digital Living Lifeform is a greenfield research-and-production program building a persistent, local-first Android artificial organism that is experienced as a living individual rather than a chatbot, scripted virtual pet, animation, or conventional game agent.

The finished system is an original full-body digital creature with a continuing identity and an internal life of its own. It maintains bounded internal physiology and motivations, initiates meaningful behavior without user prompts, learns from actual outcomes, develops persistent preferences, habits, expectations, conditioned fears and skills, remembers meaningful experiences while forgetting or consolidating redundant ones, forms differentiated relationships with known people, changes developmentally through lived experience rather than elapsed time, remains recognizably the same individual across process death, reboot, long absence, application updates, storage faults and qualified recovery, remains behaviorally complete offline and without a language model, communicates only from its actual state and history, and exhibits coherent but non-scripted individuality shaped by its life experiences.

The objective is not to simulate every biological mechanism. The objective is the minimum bounded artificial-organism architecture capable of producing a convincing subjective impression that there is a persistent individual living inside the device. Apparent life must emerge from actual persistent mechanisms rather than narrative fabrication or animation tricks, arising from the interaction of physiology and internal needs, motivational state, modulators and temperament, action priorities and commitments, curiosity, habituation and sensitization, learned preferences, conditioned avoidance and fear, habits and outcome expectations, autobiographical memory, semantic knowledge derived from experience, relationship history, developmental state, and bounded domain-separated randomness. Visual polish may strengthen the effect of life but may never substitute for the organism mechanisms themselves.

## Observable success measures

- Alive — human evidence shows the released organism exceeds the preregistered aliveness threshold against a strong, independently qualified scripted-pet baseline.
- Individual — different life histories produce observably different individuals for traceable learned reasons rather than cosmetic randomness.
- Autonomous — the organism initiates meaningful behavior from its own state without waiting for user commands.
- Adaptive — preferences, habits, fears, expectations, memories and relationships change from actual outcomes.
- Continuous — history and identity survive ordinary lifecycle and recovery events without duplication, fabrication or silent loss.
- Deterministic — identical canonical state plus identical ordered normalized evidence reproduce byte-identical canonical outcomes across the qualified JVM and Android hardware matrix.
- Embodied — the creature has a coherent body, tactile semantics and environmental interaction while remaining independent of optional AR and presentation assets.
- Local-first — its life does not depend on cloud services, remote inference or continuous Android background execution.
- Explainable — material behavior traces to the actual internal state, learning, memories, relationships, evidence and bounded random domains responsible for it.
- Bounded — physiology, learning, memory, time reconciliation and resource use cannot grow or destabilize without bounds.
- Long-lived — future versions and migrations preserve the historical organism rather than replacing it.
- Original — creature design, assets, names, presentation and mechanics remain independent original work rather than franchise imitation.
- Relationship and person differentiation is proven only by its own longitudinal qualification evidence, never inferred from a short aliveness study.

## Scope

- A deterministic canonical organism core owning identity, logical time, physiology, motivations, current commitments, learned values, memory, relationships and development, with transitions that are deterministic, fixed-point or integer based, single-threaded, explicitly ordered, bounded, versioned, replayable and durably recoverable.
- Android as host, sensor and evidence source, persistence environment and presentation surface only.
- Continuous organism semantics achieved through durable canonical state plus trusted elapsed-time evidence plus deterministic reconciliation, not an assumed immortal background process.
- A canonical environmental abstraction (AffordanceWorld) of stable normalized concepts such as resources and remedies, rest locations, safe locations, social targets, play objects, inspectable or novel objects, threats, known or unknown reachability, blocked affordances, route and risk evidence, and remembered targets and outcomes.
- A bundled virtual habitat capable of supporting the entire baseline organism.
- Spatial adapters that asynchronously return coarsely normalized RouteEvidence consumed by canonical behavior.
- A protected-life model for V1 in which neglect and adverse circumstances may cause distress, critical condition, Emergency Torpor and rehabilitation, with safety, integrity and survival-critical physiology holding absolute priority over curiosity, play and ordinary social actions.
- Bounded, deterministic, explainable, evidence-driven, locally represented learning that is reversible or weakenable where biologically appropriate, covering preferences, avoidance, fear, habits, expectations, familiarity, trust, attachment-related state and skill evidence.
- Globally bounded memory that selectively retains by significance while consolidating, forgetting, reducing redundancy, preserving important long-term identity memories, preserving provenance and confidence, and measurably influencing future behavior.
- Relationships emerging from repeated evidence and history, where a single interaction cannot manufacture deep attachment or trust.
- Development driven primarily by lived experience, with life-stage and evolution transitions preserving the same historical organism.
- A parallel disposable aliveness research track (A000 spike, A001 gate) with a deterministic accelerated simulator, matched real-time interaction viewer, identical presentation across cohorts, an independently qualified strong scripted baseline, blinded interaction, preregistered success criteria, bounded participant and power requirements, a bounded three-attempt program limit, causal decision traces, coalition and Shapley mechanism attribution, controlled human ablations and independent governance.

## Non-goals

- Simulating every biological mechanism, or adopting complex learning mechanisms that do not outperform simpler ones under the project's controlled ablation rules.
- Permanent death through ordinary neglect in V1.
- Paid survival resources or loot-box survival mechanics.
- Mandatory cloud connectivity, remote inference, or continuous Android background execution as a correctness dependency.
- Simultaneous multi-device live organism state, or automatic save roaming.
- A language model controlling the organism. A generative language model may eventually exist only as a read-only verbalization layer that converts already-canonical state into richer natural language, and may never create or modify physiology, motivations, emotions, memories, relationship facts, development, actions, rewards, identity or environmental evidence.
- Self-modifying executable organism code, or unbounded parameter mutation or learning.
- Mandatory biometrics, AR, camera or microphone use.
- A canonical general-purpose rigidbody physics engine.
- Canonical execution of A*, NavMesh search or device-specific pathfinding, or admission of raw floating-point route cost into canonical state.
- Real-world room reconstruction, depth sensing and richer spatial simulation as foundational dependencies for autonomy, learning, memory, relationships, care or development.
- Copied or derivative franchise characters or assets.
- Reboot farming, wall-clock progression exploits, delayed punishment bombs, hidden development rewards, hidden relationship progression, or converting user care into punishment.
- Inheriting code, assets or design from prior projects. This program is greenfield.

## Constraints

- Canonical authority — the deterministic organism core owns truth. Android lifecycle callbacks, renderer state, device-specific sensor noise, operating-system scheduling and presentation timing are not canonical organism truth.
- Determinism — identical canonical starting state plus identical ordered normalized evidence must produce byte-identical canonical results across the qualified JVM and Android hardware matrix.
- Local-first — the organism must remain behaviorally complete without cloud connectivity, continuous background execution, remote inference, a language model, camera permission, microphone permission, AR support, or optional Play Asset Delivery content.
- Continuity — material interactions cannot become semantically visible before their required durable boundary, recovery cannot invent missing history, and a recovered organism must preserve known history and explicitly represent any genuine recovery gap.
- Research gate — the production architecture must not assume its mechanisms actually produce perceived life. A001 can fail, and three failed scored attempts under the same foundational organism hypothesis cause ALIVENESS_PROGRAM_STOP. Production organism phases do not continue merely because substantial engineering work has already been invested.
- Execution gates as of the last verified date — R000 greenfield project initialization is authorized now; A000 aliveness spike harness and study scaffolding may proceed in parallel; the next production hard gate is the DeterminismContractV1 freeze followed by R001 deterministic fixed-point and replay qualification; R002 continuity and durability may proceed only after R001 passes; R003 through R009 production organism mechanisms are additionally blocked until A001 passes.
- No guessing — unresolved specification questions must be raised as BLOCKED_SPEC_* items rather than resolved by assumption.
- The canonical architecture supersedes conflicting implementation prose.
